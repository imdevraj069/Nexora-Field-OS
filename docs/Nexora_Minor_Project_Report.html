Abstract

On-ground field execution across industries such as construction, solar energy, telecom rollout, and infrastructure audits suffers from severe operational fragmentation. Organizations routinely rely on disconnected desk-oriented task trackers, unstructured WhatsApp media threads, paper checklists, and manual expense spreadsheets—leading to delayed visibility, unverified work claims, and rigid workflows that fail to adapt across departments.

Nexora Field OS is a multi-tenant, shard-routed, AI-governed Field Operations Operating System delivered as a responsive web platform and installable Progressive Web App (PWA). The platform combines dynamic project-scoped Kanban workflows, typed verification checklists (CHECK, TEXT, NUMBER, FILL_BLANK, OPTIONS, MULTI_SELECT, FILE, MEDIA, LINK), a tenant-isolated 50 GB MinIO object storage vault, mixed-language voice-to-text field reporting powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash, cross-organization project collaboration, and an integrated project budget and reimbursement ledger.

Technologically, Nexora employs a polyglot microservice architecture behind an NGINX reverse proxy and Cloudflare Tunnel edge layer, featuring gRPC inter-service communication, RabbitMQ asynchronous job queues, Redis caching, MongoDB unstructured AI storage, and horizontal PostgreSQL sharding (S1, S2, S3) for strict tenant isolation. End-to-end validation demonstrates sub-120ms gateway routing, zero-loss session continuity via rotating JWT refresh tokens, and automated AI timeliness and evidence-completeness scoring.

Keywords: Multi-Tenant SaaS, Horizontal Database Sharding, Field Operations OS, Progressive Web App (PWA), AI Governance, MinIO Object Storage.

1. Introduction

1.1 Background and Significance

Modern infrastructure and field-service enterprises coordinate distributed teams across geographically dispersed job sites, vendor partners, and finance controllers. Unlike software teams working at desks, field engineers operate in low-connectivity zones, capture physical proof (photos, videos, torque/voltage readings, voice notes), and incur on-site material or travel expenses. Bridging the gap between physical field execution and real-time executive oversight is critical for operational accountability.

1.2 Pain Points & Problem Statement

  1. Rigid, Hardcoded Workflows: Traditional trackers force every department into static status columns, failing to match real site lifecycles.
  2. Unverified Proof of Work: Photos and updates sent over informal chat apps lack structure, task binding, and searchable archival.
  3. Language & Friction Barriers: Field technicians prefer speaking in Hindi, Hinglish, or regional dialects rather than typing long forms on mobile screens.
  4. Siloed Cross-Organization Execution: Prime contractors and partner vendors cannot securely collaborate on shared milestones without exposing unrelated tenant data.
  5. Disconnected Project Finance: Budgets, vendor purchase orders, and technician reimbursement claims live in separate sheets detached from task progress.

1.3 Objectives of the Project

  • Business Objective: Deliver a unified vertical SaaS OS that turns on-ground execution into auditable proof and real-time financial control.
  • Technical Objective: Architect a horizontally sharded (S1–S3), microservice-driven platform with RBAC flags, 50 GB MinIO media isolation, Gemini 2.5 multimodal AI governance, and an installable 5-tab mobile/tablet PWA.

1.4 Scope of the Work

The system encompasses multi-organization onboarding, custom role/permission management, AI Lead-to-Plan project generation, milestone and Kanban task execution, versatile checklist validation, MinIO media vault management, cross-organization project invitations and task assignment, AI progress/voice analysis, and project budget/reimbursement workflows.

2. Literature Review

2.1 Summary of Existing Systems

  • Generic Task Trackers (Jira, Trello, Asana): Built for office knowledge workers; lack typed field checklists, native object-storage vaults, mixed-language voice AI, and integrated reimbursement workflows.
  • Legacy Enterprise ERPs (SAP, Oracle Primavera): Heavy desktop-centric interfaces with high licensing costs, slow mobile adoption, and rigid schema customization.
  • Informal Messaging & Spreadsheets (WhatsApp + Excel): Zero structured audit trail, no RBAC, and frequent loss of receipts or site photos.

2.3 Unique Selling Propositions (USPs) & Justification

  • Zero Hardcoded Workflows: Admins set org defaults; Project Managers customize Kanban columns and milestones per project.
  • Strict Proof-Gated Completion: Tasks cannot transition to DONE/VERIFIED until required readings, blanks, options, and MinIO file slots are fulfilled.
  • Shard-Isolated Multi-Tenancy: Organizations route dynamically to dedicated PostgreSQL shards (S1, S2, S3).
  • Cross-Org Federation: Owner orgs invite partner orgs, partners assign specialists, and PMs assign tasks across all participating organizations.

4. Implementation

4.1 Modules and Service Descriptions

  • Identity Service: Multi-org onboarding, RBAC flags, 2-hour JWT access tokens, rotating 7-day refresh tokens (POST /api/v1/auth/refresh), and gRPC GetUserProjectAccess.
  • Gateway Service: REST, GraphQL, and Socket.IO endpoints; strict checklist completion guards; cross-org invitations (project_org_collaborations) and unified project assignees; project budget, expense, and reimbursement controllers.
  • Storage Service: MinIO S3 integration enforcing 50 GB org quotas, 10-minute signed URLs, and archived-project deletion locks.
  • AI Governance Service: RabbitMQ consumer and gRPC server invoking Gemini 2.5 Flash for project blueprints, progress synthesis, and mixed-language audio analysis.
  • Frontend Web & PWA: Next.js 15 responsive workspace with Light/Dark themes, 5-button mobile PWA dock, interactive monthly calendar, and automatic 401 token-refresh fetch interceptor.

4.2 Challenges Faced During Development

  • Token Expiry During Long Field Sessions: Resolved via DB-backed refresh token rotation and a transparent 401-retry fetch interceptor in nexoraClient.ts.
  • Cross-Org Task Assignment Across Shards: Resolved via project_org_collaborations in nexora_global and unified assignee resolution in ProjectService.listProjectAssignees.
  • Theme Contrast Isolation: Resolved via [data-surface="dark"] and [data-surface="light"] CSS rules preserving contrast across both themes.

5.2 Business & Pitch Interpretation

From a business and pitch standpoint, Nexora Field OS consolidates task tracking, proof storage, voice-to-text field reporting, and project finance into a single vertical operating system—eliminating informal WhatsApp reporting and providing auditable accountability for multi-organization infrastructure projects.

6. Conclusion

6.1 Summary of Outcomes & Achievements vs. Objectives

Nexora Field OS fulfills all core business and technical objectives: dynamic per-project Kanban workflows, 9-type proof-gated checklists, cross-organization project collaboration, 50 GB MinIO media governance, Gemini 2.5 voice/progress AI analysis, rotating JWT session persistence, and an installable mobile/tablet PWA.

6.2 Limitations

  • Voice transcription accuracy in high-noise industrial sites depends on client microphone clarity.
  • Full offline IndexedDB mutation queueing (beyond cached PWA shell and session persistence) is planned for the next iteration.

7. Future Scope

  1. Offline-First Mutation Queue: IndexedDB synchronization for zero-connectivity underground or remote sites.
  2. GPS Geofencing & EXIF Verification: Automated polygon checks ensuring site photos match project coordinates.
  3. ERP & Accounting Exports: Direct GST/Tally export of approved vendor expenses and reimbursement claims.
  4. Predictive SLA Risk Forecasting: Early warning alerts 48 hours prior to projected milestone slippage.
